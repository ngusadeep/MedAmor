**Version:** 3.2
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** March 2026

## Purpose
Evidence-based protocol for COVID-19 patient management in hospital settings.

## Patient Triage
- Mild: Home isolation with monitoring
- Moderate: Hospital admission required
- Severe: ICU admission
- Critical: Mechanical ventilation support

## Treatment Guidelines
### Antiviral Therapy
- Remdesivir for hospitalized patients
- Molnupiravir for high-risk outpatients
- Paxlovid for eligible patients

### Immunomodulators
- Dexamethasone for severe cases
- Tocilizumab for cytokine storm
- Baricitinib as alternative

## Monitoring Parameters
- Oxygen saturation
- Respiratory rate
- Vital signs every 4 hours
- Laboratory markers (CRP, D-dimer, ferritin)

## Discharge Criteria
- Clinical improvement
- No fever for 24 hours
- Oxygen saturation > 94% on room air
- Two negative PCR tests 24 hours apart

## Infection Control
- PPE requirements by patient category
- Visitor restrictions
- Environmental cleaning protocols
- Staff testing protocols
