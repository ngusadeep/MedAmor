# Diabetes Management Protocol

**Version:** 2.1
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
This protocol outlines the standardized approach for managing diabetes mellitus in hospitalized patients and outpatients.

## Scope
Applies to all healthcare providers managing patients with Type 1, Type 2, and gestational diabetes.

## Key Guidelines

### 1. Diagnosis Criteria
- HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
- Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL
- 2-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during OGTT
- Random plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with symptoms

### 2. Initial Assessment
- Complete medical history
- Physical examination
- Laboratory investigations
- Cardiovascular risk assessment

### 3. Treatment Approaches
- Lifestyle modifications
- Oral hypoglycemic agents
- Insulin therapy
- Regular monitoring

## Monitoring and Follow-up
- Regular HbA1c monitoring
- Self-monitoring of blood glucose
- Annual comprehensive foot exam
- Regular eye examinations

## Emergency Protocols
- Hypoglycemia management
- Hyperglycemia management
- Diabetic ketoacidosis
- Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state
