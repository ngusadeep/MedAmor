## Purpose
Standardized diagnostic criteria for common medical conditions to ensure consistency in diagnosis and treatment.

## Cardiovascular Diseases

### Acute Coronary Syndrome
- Chest pain characteristics
- ECG changes
- Cardiac biomarkers elevation
- Risk stratification

### Heart Failure
- NYHA Classification
- LVEF criteria
- BNP/NT-proBNP levels
- Echocardiographic findings

## Respiratory Conditions

### Community-Acquired Pneumonia
- Clinical features
- CURB-65 score
- Imaging criteria
- Microbiological investigations

### COPD Exacerbation
- Anthonisen criteria
- Spirometry parameters
- Blood gas analysis
- Treatment response

## Neurological Disorders

### Stroke Classification
- Ischemic vs Hemorrhagic
- NIHSS scoring
- TOAST classification
- Imaging protocols

### Seizure Disorders
- Classification systems
- EEG criteria
- Diagnostic workup
- Differential diagnosis

## Endocrine Disorders

### Thyroid Disorders
- TSH, T3, T4 reference ranges
- Autoimmune markers
- Imaging criteria
- Treatment thresholds
