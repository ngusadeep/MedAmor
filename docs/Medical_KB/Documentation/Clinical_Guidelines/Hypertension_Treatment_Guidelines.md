# Hypertension Treatment Guidelines

**Version:** 1.8
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
Standardized treatment protocol for hypertension management in clinical practice.

## Classification
- Normal: < 120/80 mmHg
- Elevated: 120-129 (systolic) and < 80 (diastolic)
- Stage 1: 130-139/80-89 mmHg
- Stage 2: ≥ 140/90 mmHg
- Hypertensive Crisis: > 180/120 mmHg

## Treatment Algorithm
1. Lifestyle modifications for all patients
2. Pharmacological treatment based on risk stratification
3. Regular monitoring and adjustment

## First-Line Medications
- ACE inhibitors
- ARBs
- Calcium channel blockers
- Thiazide diuretics

## Monitoring
- Home blood pressure monitoring
- Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring
- Regular clinic visits
- Target achievement assessment

## Special Considerations
- Pregnancy
- Chronic kidney disease
- Diabetes mellitus
- Elderly patients
