# Infection Control Policy

**Version:** 2.4
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
To establish comprehensive infection prevention and control measures to protect patients, staff, and visitors.

## Scope
Applies to all hospital personnel, patients, visitors, and contractors working within hospital premises.

## Key Principles

### Standard Precautions
- Hand hygiene protocols
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) usage
- Respiratory hygiene
- Safe injection practices
- Environmental cleaning

### Transmission-Based Precautions
- Contact precautions
- Droplet precautions
- Airborne precautions
- Protective environment

## Hand Hygiene

### When to Perform Hand Hygiene
- Before touching a patient
- Before clean/aseptic procedures
- After body fluid exposure risk
- After touching patient surroundings
- Before eating or handling food

### Hand Hygiene Methods
- Alcohol-based hand rub (preferred)
- Soap and water (when hands visibly soiled)
- Surgical hand antisepsis

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

### PPE Selection Criteria
- Risk assessment based on procedure
- Type of anticipated exposure
- Patient's infection status
- Environmental factors

### PPE Usage Guidelines
- Donning and doffing procedures
- Proper fit and function
- Disposal protocols
- Storage requirements

## Environmental Cleaning

### Routine Cleaning
- Patient care areas
- Common areas
- Equipment surfaces
- High-touch surfaces

### Disinfection Protocols
- EPA-registered disinfectants
- Contact time requirements
- Surface compatibility
- Documentation procedures
