# Patient Frequently Asked Questions

**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Version:** 1.6

## Appointments and Scheduling

### Q: How do I schedule an appointment?
**A:** Call our scheduling line at (555) 123-4567, use our online patient portal, or visit in person. Same-day appointments available for urgent needs.

### Q: What should I bring to my appointment?
**A:** Bring your insurance card, photo ID, list of current medications, and any relevant medical records. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork.

### Q: How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
**A:** Call at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day cancellations may result in a fee. Reschedule through our portal or by calling.

### Q: What are your office hours?
**A:** Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM. Emergency care available 24/7 through our emergency department.

## Billing and Insurance

### Q: Do you accept my insurance?
**A:** We accept most major insurance plans. Call our billing department at (555) 987-6543 to verify coverage before your visit.

### Q: How do I pay my bill?
**A:** Pay online through our patient portal, by phone with a credit card, or in person at our billing office. Payment plans available for balances over $200.

### Q: What if I can't afford my medical bills?
**A:** We offer financial assistance programs, payment plans, and charity care for qualifying patients. Contact our financial counselor at (555) 456-7890.

## Medical Records and Results

### Q: How do I access my medical records?
**A:** Request records through our patient portal or by calling medical records at (555) 321-0987. Allow 5-7 business days for processing.

### Q: How do I get my test results?
**A:** Most results are available through our patient portal within 3-5 days. Your provider will call for urgent or abnormal results.

### Q: Can I get copies of my records?
**A:** Yes, we can provide copies on paper, CD, or secure electronic format. Fees may apply for extensive records.

## Medications and Prescriptions

### Q: How do I refill my prescription?
**A:** Use our patient portal, call your pharmacy directly, or contact your provider's office. Allow 2-3 days for processing.

### Q: What should I do if I have side effects from my medication?
**A:** Contact your prescribing provider immediately. For emergencies, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.

### Q: Can I get my prescriptions filled here?
**A:** We have an on-site pharmacy for your convenience. Medications can also be sent to your preferred pharmacy.

## Health and Wellness

### Q: How do I get preventive care services?
**A:** Schedule a wellness visit annually. We offer vaccinations, screenings, and counseling for preventive health.

### Q: What health education resources do you offer?
**A:** We provide classes, workshops, and materials on diabetes, heart health, nutrition, and more. Check our patient portal for schedules.

### Q: How do I get a referral to a specialist?
**A:** Your primary care provider can provide referrals. Some services don't require referrals - call to check requirements.

## Hospital Services

### Q: How do I find a patient in the hospital?
**A:** Call the hospital operator at (555) 000-0000 and ask for the patient's room number. Visiting hours are 8 AM - 8 PM daily.

### Q: What should I bring when admitted to the hospital?
**A:** Comfortable clothing, toiletries, medications in original bottles, insurance cards, and advance directive if you have one.

### Q: How do I get information about a patient's condition?
**A:** Designated family members can get updates. Ask about our patient representative program for complex cases.

## Privacy and Rights

### Q: How do you protect my health information?
**A:** We follow HIPAA regulations. Your information is secure and only shared with your permission or as required by law.

### Q: What are my patient rights?
**A:** You have the right to respectful care, information about your treatment, participation in decisions, and complaint procedures. A full list is available in our patient handbook.

### Q: How do I file a complaint?
**A:** Contact our patient advocate at (555) 654-3210 or use our online complaint form. All complaints are investigated promptly.

## Technology and Communication

### Q: How do I use the patient portal?
**A:** Register at our website with your date of birth and medical record number. You can view records, schedule appointments, and message your provider.

### Q: Can I communicate with my doctor via email?
**A:** Yes, through our secure patient portal. Response time is typically 1-2 business days. Not for emergencies.

### Q: Do you offer telemedicine visits?
**A:** Yes, for follow-up visits and consultations. Schedule through our portal or call for availability.

## Emergency and Urgent Care

### Q: When should I go to the emergency room?
**A:** For chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, suspected stroke, or any life-threatening situation. Call 911 immediately.

### Q: What is the difference between urgent care and emergency room?
**A:** Urgent care for non-life-threatening conditions like minor injuries or infections. Emergency room for serious or life-threatening conditions.

### Q: How do I know if my condition is an emergency?
**A:** If you think it might be serious, it's better to be safe and go to the emergency room or call 911.

## Support Services

### Q: Do you offer transportation services?
**A:** We can help arrange transportation for patients with medical needs. Contact our social services department at (555) 789-0123.

### Q: What interpreter services do you offer?
**A:** Professional interpreters available in multiple languages. Video remote interpreting also available 24/7.

### Q: How can I get help with social services needs?
**A:** Our social workers can help with housing, food assistance, counseling, and other support services. Call (555) 234-5678.
