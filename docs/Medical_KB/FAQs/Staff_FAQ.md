# Staff Frequently Asked Questions

**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Version:** 1.4

## Human Resources

### Q: How do I request time off?
**A:** Submit time-off requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for vacation, or as soon as possible for sick leave. Include dates, reason, and any coverage arrangements.

### Q: What are the benefits enrollment periods?
**A:** Open enrollment occurs annually from November 1-30. Life changes (marriage, birth, etc.) allow for special enrollment periods within 30 days of the event.

### Q: How do I update my personal information?
**A:** Contact HR at ext. 2100 or update through the employee self-service portal. Changes include address, phone, emergency contacts, and tax withholding.

## Clinical Operations

### Q: How do I access patient records?
**A:** Use the EHR system with your assigned login credentials. Access is limited to patients under your care and requires proper authorization levels.

### Q: What should I do if I suspect a medication error?
**A:** Immediately report to your supervisor and the pharmacy. Document the incident in the incident reporting system and follow the medication error protocol.

### Q: How do I handle a difficult patient situation?
**A:** Remain calm and professional. If safety is threatened, call security (ext. 911). Document the incident and discuss with your supervisor afterward.

## Technology and IT

### Q: I'm having trouble logging into the EHR system.
**A:** Reset your password through the IT help desk (ext. 1234) or use the "Forgot Password" link. Ensure your account isn't locked due to failed attempts.

### Q: How do I report a computer or equipment issue?
**A:** Submit a ticket through the IT service desk portal or call ext. 1234. Include your location, equipment details, and a description of the problem.

### Q: Can I use my personal device for work?
**A:** Personal devices may be used if they meet security standards and are approved through IT. Company-provided devices are recommended for sensitive work.

## Safety and Security

### Q: What should I do in case of a fire alarm?
**A:** Follow the RACE protocol: Rescue patients, Activate alarm, Contain fire, Extinguish if safe. Evacuate using nearest safe exit and report to assembly area.

### Q: How do I report a safety concern?
**A:** Use the anonymous safety reporting system or contact your supervisor. All reports are investigated and addressed confidentially.

### Q: What are the procedures for handling sharps?
**A:** Never recap needles. Dispose immediately in designated sharps containers. Report any needlestick injuries immediately and seek medical evaluation.

## Patient Care

### Q: How do I handle a patient complaint?
**A:** Listen actively, apologize for any inconvenience, and escalate to the patient advocate (ext. 3000) if needed. Document the complaint and resolution.

### Q: What are the visitation policies?
**A:** General visiting hours are 8 AM - 8 PM. Special accommodations can be made for end-of-life care or pediatric patients. COVID-19 restrictions may apply.

### Q: How do I transfer a patient to another facility?
**A:** Complete the transfer paperwork, ensure medical records accompany the patient, coordinate with receiving facility, and document the transfer in the EHR.

## Administrative

### Q: How do I submit an expense report?
**A:** Use the expense reporting system in the finance portal. Attach receipts, include business purpose, and submit within 30 days of the expense.

### Q: What is the policy on cell phone use?
**A:** Personal cell phones should be on silent mode. Business use is permitted in break areas. No cell phones in patient care areas except for emergencies.

### Q: How do I access the staff break room?
**A:** Use your employee ID badge to access the break room. Keep the area clean and respect quiet hours during meal times.

## Emergency Procedures

### Q: What is the code for cardiac arrest?
**A:** Code Blue. Call overhead immediately and begin CPR if trained. Designated team will respond with emergency equipment.

### Q: How do I activate the rapid response team?
**A:** Call ext. 2222 or press the rapid response button. Provide patient location and brief description of the concern.

### Q: What should I do if I witness workplace violence?
**A:** Remove yourself and others from danger. Call security (ext. 911) immediately. Report the incident through proper channels afterward.

## Professional Development

### Q: How do I enroll in continuing education?
**A:** Check the learning management system for available courses. Many are online and self-paced. Contact education department for in-person sessions.

### Q: What certifications are required for my role?
**A:** Check your job description and the credentialing database. Common requirements include BLS, ACLS, and specialty-specific certifications.

### Q: How do I apply for a promotion or transfer?
**A:** Submit an internal application through the HR portal. Include your resume, references, and a statement of interest. Discuss with your supervisor first.

**For questions not covered here, contact your supervisor or the appropriate department.**
