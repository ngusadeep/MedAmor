# Medical Records Management Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 1.8
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
Systematic management of medical records to ensure accuracy, accessibility, and compliance.

## Scope
Applies to all patient medical record creation, maintenance, storage, and disposition.

## Record Creation and Documentation

### Documentation Standards
- Timely and accurate entries
- Legible handwriting or electronic entry
- Complete patient identification
- Date and time stamping

### Content Requirements
- Chief complaint and history
- Physical examination findings
- Diagnostic test results
- Treatment plans and progress notes
- Discharge summaries

## Record Maintenance

### File Organization
- Chronological ordering
- Section dividers usage
- Document attachment procedures
- Missing document identification

### Update Procedures
- Amendment processes
- Correction protocols
- Addendum procedures
- Authentication requirements

## Access and Security

### Access Authorization
- Role-based access controls
- User authentication procedures
- Audit trail maintenance
- Access log monitoring

### Confidentiality Protection
- Privacy screen usage
- Document handling procedures
- Unauthorized access prevention
- Breach reporting protocols

## Storage and Retention

### Active Storage
- Current patient records location
- Retrieval procedures
- File tracking systems
- Security measures

### Archival Storage
- Long-term storage facilities
- Climate-controlled environments
- Inventory management
- Retrieval protocols

## Retention Schedules

### Adult Patient Records
- Inpatient records: 7 years post-discharge
- Outpatient records: 7 years after last visit
- Minor records: 7 years after age 18 or 21 years after last visit

### Special Categories
- Obstetric records: 21 years
- Cancer records: Lifetime retention
- Research records: Per protocol requirements

## Disposal Procedures

### Record Destruction
- Authorization requirements
- Secure destruction methods
- Witness documentation
- Certificate of destruction

### Electronic Disposal
- Data wiping procedures
- Hard drive destruction
- Backup tape disposal
- Verification processes
