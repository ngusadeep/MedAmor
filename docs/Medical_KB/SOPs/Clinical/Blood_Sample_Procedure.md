# Blood Sample Collection Procedure

**Version:** 1.7
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** July 2026

## Purpose
Standardized procedure for safe and accurate blood sample collection.

## Scope
Applies to all blood collection procedures performed by authorized healthcare personnel.

## Equipment and Supplies

### Required Equipment
- Blood collection tubes (various types)
- Tourniquet
- Alcohol swabs
- Gauze pads
- Adhesive bandages
- Sharps container

### Personal Protective Equipment
- Gloves (non-sterile)
- Laboratory coat
- Face shield (if splash risk)
- Hand hygiene supplies

## Patient Preparation

### Patient Identification
1. Verify patient identity using two identifiers
2. Confirm date of birth and medical record number
3. Check allergy status
4. Review medication history

### Site Selection and Preparation
- Assess vein visibility and accessibility
- Avoid compromised sites (bruises, IV lines, surgical sites)
- Clean site with alcohol swab using circular motion
- Allow to air dry completely

## Collection Procedure

### Venipuncture Technique
1. Apply tourniquet 3-4 inches above intended site
2. Anchor vein by pulling skin taut
3. Insert needle at 15-30 degree angle
4. Observe for blood return in flashback chamber
5. Release tourniquet before removing needle

### Tube Filling
- Fill tubes in correct order
- Gently invert tubes immediately after filling
- Label tubes at bedside
- Place in biohazard transport bags

## Post-Collection Care

### Site Care
- Apply pressure with gauze for 2-3 minutes
- Apply adhesive bandage
- Instruct patient on care of puncture site
- Monitor for signs of complications

### Documentation
- Record collection time and date
- Document site used and complications
- Note special circumstances
- Update laboratory requisition

## Special Considerations

### Difficult Venipuncture
- Use smaller gauge needles
- Apply warm compresses
- Use vein finder devices
- Consider alternative sites

### Pediatric Collections
- Use age-appropriate techniques
- Minimize trauma and pain
- Use distraction techniques
- Involve parents appropriately

### Geriatric Considerations
- Account for fragile skin
- Use gentle technique
- Provide adequate support
- Monitor for orthostatic hypotension

## Quality Control

### Specimen Integrity
- Proper labeling verification
- Correct tube type usage
- Appropriate filling volumes
- Timely transport to laboratory

### Infection Control
- Hand hygiene compliance
- PPE usage
- Sharps safety
- Surface decontamination

## Emergency Procedures

### Vasovagal Response
- Recognize symptoms (pallor, sweating, bradycardia)
- Lower patient head and elevate feet
- Monitor vital signs
- Provide reassurance and support

### Hematoma Formation
- Apply immediate pressure
- Elevate extremity
- Apply cold compress
- Monitor for expansion

## Training and Competency

### Initial Training
- Theory instruction
- Supervised practice
- Competency assessment
- Certification requirements

### Ongoing Competency
- Annual skill validation
- Continuing education
- Error rate monitoring
- Performance improvement
