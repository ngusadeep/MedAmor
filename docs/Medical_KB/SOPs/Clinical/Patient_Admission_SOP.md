# Patient Admission Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 2.1
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** July 2026

## Purpose
To ensure efficient, safe, and standardized patient admission process.

## Scope
Applies to all inpatient admissions, emergency admissions, and scheduled procedures requiring admission.

## Pre-Admission Phase

### Appointment Scheduling
1. Patient registration verification
2. Insurance pre-authorization
3. Pre-admission testing coordination
4. Financial counseling if needed

### Pre-Admission Documentation
- Medical history forms
- Medication reconciliation
- Advance directives
- Consent forms preparation

## Admission Process

### Patient Arrival
1. Welcome and identification verification
2. Registration completion
3. Insurance verification
4. Consent forms signing

### Initial Assessment
- Vital signs measurement
- Pain assessment
- Fall risk assessment
- Nutritional screening

### Room Assignment
- Acuity-based bed assignment
- Isolation requirements assessment
- Special needs accommodation
- Room preparation verification

## Clinical Documentation

### Admission Orders
- Admission diagnosis
- Vital signs frequency
- Activity level
- Diet orders
- Medication reconciliation

### Nursing Assessment
- Comprehensive physical assessment
- Baseline measurements
- Patient education needs
- Discharge planning initiation

## Communication and Handoff

### Team Notification
- Attending physician notification
- Primary nurse assignment
- Ancillary services coordination
- Family communication

### Shift Handoff
- SBAR communication format
- Critical information transfer
- Pending orders review
- Care plan discussion

## Quality Assurance

### Documentation Standards
- Timeliness requirements
- Accuracy verification
- Completeness checks
- Regulatory compliance

### Patient Safety Checks
- Identification verification
- Allergy confirmation
- Medication reconciliation
- Fall prevention measures

## Post-Admission Care

### Immediate Care Needs
- Pain management
- Comfort measures
- Mobility assistance
- Nutritional support

### Ongoing Assessment
- Vital signs monitoring
- Patient response evaluation
- Care plan adjustment
- Family involvement
