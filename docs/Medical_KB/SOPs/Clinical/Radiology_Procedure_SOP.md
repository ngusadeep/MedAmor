# Radiology Procedure Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 1.9
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** July 2026

## Purpose
Standardized procedures for safe and effective radiology examinations.

## Scope
Applies to all diagnostic imaging procedures performed in radiology department.

## Patient Preparation

### Pre-Procedure Assessment
1. Verify patient identity and examination order
2. Review medical history and contraindications
3. Assess pregnancy status for women of childbearing age
4. Confirm NPO status for applicable examinations

### Patient Education
- Explain procedure and expectations
- Answer questions and address concerns
- Obtain informed consent
- Provide preparation instructions

## Examination Protocols

### General Radiography
- Patient positioning verification
- Radiation protection measures
- Image quality assessment
- Repeat image minimization

### Computed Tomography (CT)
- Contrast media administration protocols
- Radiation dose optimization
- Patient monitoring during scan
- Post-contrast care instructions

### Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
- Metal screening procedures
- Claustrophobia assessment
- Contrast administration safety
- Acoustic noise protection

## Radiation Safety

### ALARA Principle
- Time minimization
- Distance maximization
- Shielding utilization
- Dose monitoring and recording

### Staff Protection
- Personal dosimeter usage
- Protective apron and thyroid shield
- Radiation monitoring badges
- Pregnancy declaration policies

## Contrast Media Administration

### Preparation
- Allergy history assessment
- Renal function evaluation
- Premedication protocols
- Emergency equipment readiness

### Administration
- Vital signs monitoring
- Injection technique standardization
- Flow rate control
- Post-injection observations

## Pediatric Considerations

### Age-Appropriate Care
- Child life specialist involvement
- Parental presence options
- Sedation protocols
- Immobilization techniques

### Radiation Dose Reduction
- Pediatric-specific protocols
- Organ shielding
- Scan parameter optimization
- Follow-up dose monitoring

## Emergency Procedures

### Contrast Reactions
- Recognition of reaction signs
- Immediate response protocols
- Emergency medication administration
- Documentation and reporting

### Equipment Malfunctions
- Patient safety assurance
- Alternative procedure consideration
- Equipment restart procedures
- Incident documentation

## Quality Assurance

### Image Quality Standards
- Positioning accuracy
- Technical factor appropriateness
- Artifact minimization
- Diagnostic adequacy

### Documentation Requirements
- Procedure completion verification
- Complications recording
- Follow-up recommendations
- Report correlation

## Post-Procedure Care

### Patient Monitoring
- Vital signs assessment
- Symptom evaluation
- Discharge instructions
- Follow-up appointment scheduling

### Results Communication
- Preliminary findings notification
- Final report distribution
- Critical results communication
- Referring physician notification
