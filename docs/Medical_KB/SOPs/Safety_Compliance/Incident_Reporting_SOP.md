# Incident Reporting Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 2.1
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
Systematic reporting and investigation of incidents to improve patient safety and prevent recurrence.

## Scope
Applies to all incidents occurring within hospital premises involving patients, staff, visitors, or equipment.

## Incident Classification

### Patient Safety Events
- Medication errors
- Falls and injuries
- Healthcare-associated infections
- Pressure ulcers
- Wrong patient/procedure/site events

### Security Incidents
- Assaults and threats
- Missing patients or infants
- Property damage or theft
- Unauthorized access

### Equipment and Infrastructure
- Equipment malfunction
- Utility failures
- Construction-related incidents
- Hazardous material spills

## Reporting Requirements

### Immediate Reporting
- Verbal notification to supervisor
- Patient safety assessment
- Emergency response activation
- Preliminary documentation

### Written Reporting
- Incident report completion within 24 hours
- Detailed description of events
- Contributing factors identification
- Corrective actions documentation

## Reporting Process

### Initial Assessment
1. Ensure patient/staff safety
2. Gather preliminary information
3. Notify appropriate personnel
4. Preserve incident scene if applicable

### Documentation Requirements
- Date, time, and location
- Individuals involved
- Sequence of events
- Witness information
- Immediate actions taken

## Investigation Procedures

### Investigation Team
- Department manager/supervisor
- Quality improvement coordinator
- Subject matter experts
- Patient safety officer (as needed)

### Root Cause Analysis
- Timeline development
- Contributing factor identification
- System issue assessment
- Prevention strategy development

## Corrective Actions

### Immediate Actions
- Patient care stabilization
- Equipment replacement/securing
- Environmental hazard correction
- Communication to affected parties

### Systemic Improvements
- Policy/procedure modifications
- Staff education and training
- Equipment upgrades
- Process redesign

## Follow-up and Monitoring

### Resolution Tracking
- Action item assignment
- Completion deadline establishment
- Progress monitoring
- Effectiveness evaluation

### Trend Analysis
- Incident pattern identification
- Common cause analysis
- Prevention strategy development
- Performance metric monitoring

### Protected Information

### Legal Reporting Requirements
