# Personal Protective Equipment Usage Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 1.5
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
Proper selection, usage, and disposal of personal protective equipment to prevent exposure to infectious agents.

## Scope
Applies to all healthcare personnel requiring PPE for patient care activities.

## PPE Risk Assessment

### Standard Precautions
- Gloves for all patient contact
- Gowns for anticipated splash
- Masks for droplet protection
- Eye protection for mucous membrane exposure

### Transmission-Based Precautions
- Contact precautions
- Droplet precautions
- Airborne precautions
- Protective environment

## Equipment Selection

### Glove Selection
- Examination gloves for routine care
- Surgical gloves for sterile procedures
- Double gloving for high-risk procedures
- Specialty gloves for chemical hazards

### Mask and Respirator Types
- Surgical masks for droplet protection
- N95 respirators for airborne protection
- PAPRs for extensive airborne exposure
- Face shields for splash protection

## Donning Procedures

### Sequence for Full PPE
1. Perform hand hygiene
2. Don gown (if required)
3. Don mask/respirator
4. Don eye protection
5. Don gloves
6. Perform hand hygiene

### Fit Verification
- Mask seal checking
- Glove fit assessment
- Gown coverage verification
- Comfort and mobility evaluation

## Usage Guidelines

### During Patient Care
- Avoid touching contaminated surfaces
- Change gloves between patients
- Perform hand hygiene after glove removal
- Limit movement between patient rooms

### Special Procedures
- Sterile technique maintenance
- Aseptic field protection
- Contamination prevention
- Team coordination

## Doffing Procedures

### Safe Removal Sequence
1. Gloves removal first
2. Gown removal (roll inward)
3. Eye protection removal
4. Mask/respirator removal
5. Perform hand hygiene

### Contamination Prevention
- Avoid touching outer surfaces
- Dispose in designated containers
- Surface decontamination
- Hand hygiene completion

## Maintenance and Storage

### Storage Requirements
- Clean, dry storage areas

### Decontamination Procedures

- Surface compatibility
- Verification procedures
