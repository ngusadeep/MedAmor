# Sharps Disposal Standard Operating Procedure

**Version:** 1.3
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Review Date:** January 2027

## Purpose
Safe handling and disposal of sharps to prevent needlestick injuries and exposure to bloodborne pathogens.

## Scope
Applies to all sharps usage and disposal in clinical and non-clinical areas.

## Sharps Definition

### Regulated Medical Sharps
- Hypodermic needles and syringes
- Scalpel blades
- Intravenous stylets
- Phlebotomy devices
- Lancets and other blood sampling devices

### Contaminated Sharps
- Any sharp that has contacted blood or body fluids
- Sharps from patients with bloodborne infections
- Sharps from procedures with high-risk exposure

## Handling Procedures

### Safe Handling
- Never recap needles manually
- Use one-handed technique for recapping (if required)
- Avoid bending or breaking needles
- Handle with care to prevent injury

### Transportation
- Use puncture-resistant containers
- Close containers during transport
- Label as biohazard
- Secure in secondary containers if needed

## Disposal Containers

### Container Specifications
- Puncture-resistant plastic
- Leak-proof design
- Clearly labeled as "Biohazard Sharps"
- Color-coded (typically red)
- Appropriate size for usage area

### Placement Guidelines
- Readily accessible within arm's reach
- Securely mounted to prevent tipping
- Not overfilled (fill line indicators)
- Located away from high-traffic areas

## Disposal Procedures

### Container Filling
- Fill to designated fill line only
- Do not compress or force items
- Replace when three-quarters full
- Do not remove sharps from containers

### Replacement Protocol
1. Notify appropriate personnel
2. Transport full container safely
3. Replace with empty container
4. Document replacement in log

## Waste Management

### Biohazard Waste Handling
- Treat as regulated medical waste
- Seal containers securely
- Label with biohazard symbols
- Store in designated areas

### Disposal Methods
- Incineration for regulated waste
- Autoclaving followed by shredding
- Licensed waste disposal contractors
- Documentation of disposal

## Emergency Procedures

### Needlestick Injury
1. Encourage bleeding from wound
2. Wash area with soap and water
3. Report incident immediately
4. Seek medical evaluation
5. Complete incident report

### Container Breach
- Isolate affected area
- Notify infection control
- Assess exposure risk
- Implement cleanup procedures
