# SOP: Imaging Documentation and Follow-up (Sample)

## 1. Imaging Order and Indication

- Every imaging study must have a documented **clinical indication** in the order or associated note.
- The ordering provider and location must be recorded.

## 2. Report and Result Documentation

- Radiologist or interpreting provider report must be linked to the study.
- **Critical findings** require explicit communication and documentation (e.g. read-back, note in chart).
- Result must be available in the EHR timeline (e.g. Diagnostic Report, Imaging Study status: available).

## 3. Clinical Correlation and Follow-up

- Clinical notes after the imaging date should **reference** significant imaging results where relevant.
- If follow-up imaging or referral is indicated by the report, this must be documented in a care plan or follow-up note.
- **Dissonance:** If imaging shows a significant finding but no corresponding clinical action or note is documented, this is a potential audit finding (imaging–clinical dissonance).

## 4. Dental and Other Modalities

- Same principles apply to dental X-rays and other modalities: indication, result, and clinical action/follow-up must be traceable in the record.

## 5. Audit Triggers

- Imaging study present with no linked diagnostic report or note.
- Report mentions "follow-up" or "correlation recommended" with no documented follow-up plan.
- Clinical note after imaging does not mention material imaging results when they are relevant to the encounter.
