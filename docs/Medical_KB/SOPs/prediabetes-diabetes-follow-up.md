# SOP: Prediabetes and Diabetes Follow-up (Sample)

## 1. Identification

- **Prediabetes:** HbA1c 5.7%–6.4% (or equivalent); or documented condition "Prediabetes (finding)".
- **Diabetes:** HbA1c ≥6.5% or documented diabetes diagnosis.

## 2. Follow-up and Monitoring

- Annual (or per guideline) **HbA1c** for prediabetes; more frequent for diabetes as per care plan.
- **Medication review** for diabetic medications; "medication review due" should be resolved with a documented review.
- **Care plan:** Diabetic diet, exercise therapy, or other activities should be documented when applicable.

## 3. Audit Triggers

- Prediabetes or diabetes documented but no HbA1c in the last 12 months (or per institutional policy).
- "Medication review due (situation) (active)" for a patient on diabetes-related care with no review documented.
- Care plan with "Diabetic diet" or "Exercise therapy" but no follow-up encounter addressing adherence or outcome.

## 4. Out of Scope for Audit

- The audit does **not** diagnose diabetes or prediabetes; it checks that **documented** conditions and guidelines have corresponding documentation and follow-up.
