## How to Schedule an Appointment

### Online Patient Portal
1. **Access the Portal**
   - Visit our secure patient portal website
   - Use your username and password
   - Complete two-factor authentication if enabled

2. **Navigate to Scheduling**
   - Click "Schedule Appointment" from the main menu
   - Select your preferred provider or service
   - Choose appointment type (routine, urgent, follow-up)

3. **Select Date and Time**
   - View available time slots
   - Consider your schedule and travel time
   - Check for any preparation requirements

4. **Confirm Appointment**
   - Review all appointment details
   - Add any special requests or notes
   - Receive confirmation email and calendar invite

### Phone Scheduling
1. **Call Our Scheduling Line**
   - Dial our main appointment line
   - Have your patient ID ready
   - Be prepared to provide insurance information

2. **Speak with Scheduler**
   - Clearly state your needs and preferences
   - Ask about wait times and availability
   - Inquire about any preparation needed

3. **Confirmation Process**
   - Receive verbal confirmation
   - Note the date, time, and location
   - Ask for reminder call preferences

## Preparation Guidelines

### Before Your Appointment
- **Medical Records**: Bring previous test results and medication list
- **Insurance Cards**: Have current insurance information
- **Identification**: Bring photo ID and patient card
- **Payment**: Know your copay or payment responsibilities

### Special Preparations
- **Fasting Requirements**: Some tests require no food for 8-12 hours
- **Medication Adjustments**: Follow instructions about medication timing
- **Transportation**: Arrange for someone to drive if sedation is involved
- **Clothing**: Wear comfortable, easily removable clothing for some exams

## Rescheduling and Cancellation

### Same-Day Cancellations
- Call as early as possible
- Allow time for another patient to be scheduled
- Note any urgent medical needs

### Rescheduling Procedures
- Contact scheduling office
- Provide reason for change
- Request alternative dates/times
- Confirm new appointment details

## Wait Times and Expectations

### Typical Wait Times
- Routine appointments: 1-2 weeks
- Urgent care: Same day or next day
- Emergency care: Immediate attention

### During Your Visit
- Arrive 15 minutes early for registration
- Bring all necessary forms and payments
- Expect to wait in reception area
- Be prepared for examination room wait
