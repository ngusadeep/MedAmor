## General Medication Safety

### Before Taking Medications
- **Read Labels Carefully**: Check medication name, dose, and instructions
- **Check Expiration Dates**: Never take expired medications
- **Verify Prescriptions**: Ensure correct medication and dosage
- **Storage Requirements**: Follow proper storage instructions

### Important Safety Measures
- Take medications at the same time each day
- Use pill organizers or reminder apps
- Keep medications in original containers
- Store away from children and pets

## Oral Medications

### Tablets and Capsules
1. **Preparation**
   - Wash hands thoroughly
   - Check medication label
   - Verify correct dose

2. **Administration**
   - Take with a full glass of water (8 oz)
   - Sit upright or stand
   - Swallow whole (don't crush unless directed)
   - Don't take with certain foods if specified

3. **After Taking**
   - Replace cap securely
   - Store in proper location
   - Mark calendar or use reminder system

### Liquid Medications
1. **Measurement**
   - Use proper measuring device (syringe, cup, spoon)
   - Don't use household spoons
   - Check measurement at eye level

2. **Administration**
   - Shake well if required
   - Take at room temperature
   - Follow with additional water if needed
   - Rinse measuring device immediately

## Topical Medications

### Creams and Ointments
1. **Skin Preparation**
   - Clean area with mild soap and water
   - Pat dry gently
   - Wash hands before application

2. **Application**
   - Apply thin layer to affected area
   - Rub in gently until absorbed
   - Don't cover with tight bandages unless directed
   - Wash hands after application

### Eye Drops and Ointments
1. **Preparation**
   - Wash hands thoroughly
   - Check expiration date
   - Warm drops to room temperature if cold

2. **Administration**
   - Tilt head back slightly
   - Pull lower eyelid down gently
   - Apply prescribed number of drops
   - Close eye for 1-2 minutes
   - Don't touch tip to eye or eyelid

## Inhaled Medications

### Metered-Dose Inhalers (MDI)
1. **Preparation**
   - Remove cap and shake well
   - Prime if new or not used recently
   - Breathe out fully

2. **Technique**
   - Hold inhaler 1-2 inches from mouth
   - Press down while breathing in slowly
   - Hold breath for 10 seconds
   - Wait 1 minute before second puff

### Dry Powder Inhalers (DPI)
1. **Loading Dose**
   - Open inhaler according to instructions
   - Load medication (twist, click, or slide)
   - Don't shake or prime

2. **Inhalation**
   - Breathe out away from inhaler
   - Place mouthpiece between teeth
   - Breathe in quickly and deeply
   - Hold breath for 10 seconds

## Injectable Medications

### Insulin Injections
1. **Preparation**
   - Gather supplies: insulin, syringes, alcohol swabs
   - Check insulin type and expiration
   - Wash hands thoroughly

2. **Injection Technique**
   - Clean injection site with alcohol
   - Pinch skin and insert needle at 90-degree angle
   - Inject slowly over 5-10 seconds
   - Withdraw needle and apply gentle pressure

### Pre-filled Syringes
1. **Safety Checks**
   - Verify medication and dose
   - Check for air bubbles
   - Ensure needle is secure

2. **Administration**
   - Choose appropriate injection site
   - Clean site with alcohol swab
   - Insert at proper angle
   - Inject medication steadily

## Special Considerations

### For Children
- Use child-appropriate measuring devices
- Consider taste and ease of administration
- Supervise administration when possible
- Consult pediatrician for concerns

### For Elderly Patients
- Use larger print labels and reminders
- Consider pill crushers or alternatives for swallowing difficulties
- Set up automatic refill reminders
- Use medication management services

### During Travel
- Carry medications in original containers
- Keep in carry-on luggage
- Maintain proper temperature control
- Carry extra supply for delays

## When to Seek Help

### Contact Your Provider If:
- You miss multiple doses
- Experience unexpected side effects
- Have difficulty swallowing medications
- Notice changes in medication effectiveness
- Run out of medications unexpectedly

### Emergency Situations
- Severe allergic reactions
- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain or irregular heartbeat
- Severe vomiting or diarrhea
- Uncontrolled bleeding
