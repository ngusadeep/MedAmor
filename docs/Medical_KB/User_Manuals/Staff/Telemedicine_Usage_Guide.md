- Webcam with HD resolution

- Quiet, private consultation space

## During the Visit

### Connection Process
1. Login 5 minutes before appointment
2. Test audio and video functionality
3. Verify patient identity
4. Begin consultation

### Consultation Conduct
- Maintain eye contact through camera
- Use clear, concise communication
- Employ visual aids when helpful
- Document key findings in real-time
