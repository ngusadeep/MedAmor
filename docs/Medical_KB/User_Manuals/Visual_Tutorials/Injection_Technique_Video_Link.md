# Injection Technique Instructional Video

**Title:** Safe Injection Techniques for Home Use
**Format:** Video Tutorial
**Duration:** 8 minutes 45 seconds
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026

## Video Description
Comprehensive instructional video demonstrating proper injection techniques for patients who need to administer medications at home.

## Video Contents

### Introduction (0:00 - 1:15)
- Importance of proper injection technique
- Safety precautions and hygiene
- When to contact healthcare provider

### Supplies Needed (1:15 - 2:30)
- Various syringe types and sizes
- Insulin pens and pre-filled syringes
- Alcohol swabs and sharps containers
- Proper storage containers

### Injection Sites (2:30 - 4:00)
- Upper arm (deltoid muscle)
- Thigh (vastus lateralis)
- Abdomen (avoiding belly button area)
- Buttocks (ventrogluteal site)

### Step-by-Step Injection Process (4:00 - 6:30)
1. **Preparation**
   - Hand washing and cleaning work surface
   - Checking medication expiration
   - Gathering all supplies

2. **Site Selection and Preparation**
   - Rotating injection sites
   - Cleaning site with alcohol swab
   - Allowing site to dry completely

3. **Injection Technique**
   - Proper needle insertion angle (90 or 45 degrees)
   - Slow, steady pressure application
   - Correct depth insertion
   - Smooth plunger depression

4. **After Injection Care**
   - Needle withdrawal at same angle
   - Applying gentle pressure with gauze
   - Proper disposal in sharps container
   - Site care and monitoring

### Special Considerations (6:30 - 8:00)
- Insulin injection techniques
- Pediatric injection considerations
- Elderly patient modifications
- Troubleshooting common issues

### Safety Review (8:00 - 8:45)
- Never share needles or syringes
- Proper disposal procedures
- Recognizing signs of infection
- When to seek medical attention
